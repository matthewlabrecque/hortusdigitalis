The left chair sits menacingly.
There's nothing wrong with the chair at all,
it's the same as the right one, but I just like
the right one better. But why? Perhaps in a
previous life did I die because my right side
wasn't guarded, and I have some hidden need
to protect it? I sit in the left chair, not happy at all.
