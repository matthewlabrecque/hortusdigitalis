CHRIS: "What's the difference between a nice guy and a good man?"

MATTHEW: "A nice guy gets along. 'Yeah, do that. Yeah, I'll do that.' They don't 
necessarily have discernment or judgment, not sure what they stand for or stand 
against. It's like, 'yes, yes, yes, sure, yeah, hey.' A good man has ideals that 
they stand for and they'll stand against and when they're tested, a good man is 
not a nice guy."
