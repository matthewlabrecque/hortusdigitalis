### Who is Jon Bellion
Jon Bellion is an American signer, producer, and songwriter from Dix Hills, New
York.

### Origins
Bellion's first interactions with music was in 8th grade when Jon's brother
brought home a Triton keyboard, and evne though he was told not to touc hit he
woudl sneak into his brother's bedroom to make beats which he would record on a
tape player.

In high school, Jon becameboth a prolific basketball player as well as a musician.
He actually recieved an offer to play collegiate basketball, but instead elected
to focus on his music fulltime, enrolling at Five Towns College. He would only
attend for a year before dropping out at 19 to focus on songwriting
professionally.

He worked under Kara DioGuardi at Warner Bros learning song writing, and in 2011
he would release his first mixtape "Scattered Thoughts Vol. 1", but he would
explode in popularity when he co-wrote the Rhianna/Eminem smash hit "The
Monster" which would win a Grammy Award.

### Early mixtapes
Bellion would continue to release mixtapes, including Translations Through
Speakers in 2013, and would feature on Logic's mixtape Young Sinatra: Welcome to
Forever. Unfortunately I don't have any copies of this early music on my
computer, so the first note of Bellion we're gonna observe is from his second 2013
project "The Separation". **Kingdom Come**

### Later Music, Debut Album, and Beef with Record Execs
In 2016 Bellion would release his debut album, The Human Condition whic would
peak at number 5 on the Billboard 200, driven by the double-platinum song "All
Time Low". However, I don't think that's the best song on the record, instead I
would direct you to the song 80's Films off of that Album. **80's Films**

However, Bellion would begin beefing wiht his label as well as
Live Nation for taking advantage of him and other artists, prioritizing profits
over artist financial security or happiness. I can't go into all the details
here, but he did an interview with George Janko where he talked about the music
industry, which I think is a must listen.

He would end up refusing to release music for 7 years while he fought against
his contract, but would go on to become one of hte most prolific songwriters in
modern times, writing for Lizzo, katy Perry, Justin Bieber, Camila Cabello, Jung
Kook, Jonas Brothers, Jason Derullo, Selena Gomez, Miley Cyrus, Maroon 5, Five
Seconds of Summer, Ben Platt.

In 2018 he would follow up with Glory Sound Prep, his second album which peaked
at number 15. **Blu**

### Independent Artist and 
In 2025 he would buy his way out of his contract, and begin releaseing music
again under his own label, Beautiful Mind Records. His third Album Father Figure
dropped in 2025, and he would release Two Car Garage in 2026 with Swae Lee and
FANDOM.

His music in this period is heavily shaped by his personal growth and become a
father, which can be seen in songs such as "Father Figure", "My Boy", and "Why".
